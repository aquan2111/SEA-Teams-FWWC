# MD3: Vietnam 0-7 Netherlands

**Story:** Netherlands, chasing goals to top the group, defended higher than any of Vietnam's other opponents and scored 3 goals within 12 minutes from balls won in Vietnam's half, which left Vietnam 3 or more goals down for over 80 minutes.

## Context

This was the last match of the group stage for both teams. Vietnam were already out, while Netherlands were on level points with USWNT but lower goal difference.

With this win, Netherlands topped the group, and then they went on to beat South Africa in the Round of 16. In the quarter finals, they were eliminated by the eventual champion Spain after extra time.

## Turning point

Netherlands started aggressively and were already up by 3 goals by the 18th minute and by 4 goals by the 23rd minute, which essentially sealed a victory for them. However, as they still needed more goals to ensure they topped the group, they kept on attacking and ended up with 3 more goals, with 1 at the end of the first half and another 2 in the second half.

## Goals conceded

While the 1st and 5th goal came from Netherlands build up, the 2nd to 4th goals all came after Vietnam lost the ball in their own half. The 3rd and 6th goal were similar as Esmee Brugts curled the ball from the left edge of the box to the top right corner. The 7th goal came from a free kick just over the halfway line.

## Luck

Netherlands scored 7 goals from 4.24 non penalty xG, with 2 goals from Esmee Brugts being low quality chance, which suggested that Vietnam was unfortunate to concede 3 more goals than what Netherlands' chances suggested.

## Biggest gap

The biggest gap can be seen in both teams' final third, as Netherlands were much more dominant in Vietnam's final third both in terms of entries and touches. The number of shots also reflected the dominance as Netherlands took 8 times more shots than Vietnam. In addition, Netherlands' possessions lasted longer with more completed passes.

## What went well

Vietnam tried to play more through the Dutch press with more passes and higher share of passes completed than the previous two games. Furthermore, Vietnam still managed to take 5 shots, with 4 of them coming from the first half.

## Narrative

As Netherlands needed to win with a margin as large as possible, they started quickly and got 4 goals by the 23rd minute, with 3 of them coming from gaining the ball in Vietnam's half. They picked up one more goal just before half time, before getting 2 more in the 2nd half.

## What could have been done

- With 3 goals coming from losing the ball in their own half, Vietnam needed to be more careful with turnovers in their own half and defending after these turnovers.
- While Vietnam played shorter and completed more passes than in the first two matches, they lost the ball more in their own half against the highest press they faced in this tournament. Keeping that ambition while losing the ball less often near their own goal is the main thing to look at.

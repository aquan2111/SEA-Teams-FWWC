# MD1: Vietnam 0-3 United States

**Story:** Vietnam rarely got out of their own half and could not take a shot, while USWNT got 3 goals which closely corresponded to their non penalty expected goals.

## Context

This was the first match for both teams. The USWNT came to this tournament as champions from 2015 and 2019, while 2023 was Vietnam's debut in a FIFA Women's World Cup.

The USWNT had a disappointing title defending run, with this game being their only victory in this campaign. They drew in the other two group stage games against Netherlands and Portugal, before being eliminated in the round of 16 after losing on the penalty shootout against Sweden. This was also USWNT's worst finish in a FIFA Women's World Cup.

## Turning point

Vietnam conceded their first goal at the 14th minute, and then conceded a penalty slightly before the 45th minute mark, but Alex Morgan's penalty was saved. USWNT still managed to get their 2nd goal at the 7th minute of stoppage time in the first half. The 3rd goal was scored at the 77th minute. None of these goals looked like a real turning point in this game.

## Goals conceded

Among the 3 goals conceded, the 2nd goal came off a delayed set piece, while the other 2 came from open play build ups.

## Luck

USWNT scored 3 goals from 3.17 non penalty xG, which suggested that their number of goals reflected the quality of their chances.

The result could have been 4-0 had it not been for Kim Thanh's save against Alex Morgan's penalty.

## Biggest gap

The biggest gap can be seen in how long and how far possessions got. More than half of USWNT's possessions reached Vietnam's box. Vietnam's possessions were much shorter, with two thirds lasting less than 10 seconds. On the other hand, Vietnam could not enter USWNT's box and did not manage to take any shots.

## What went well

Kim Thanh managed a save when facing Alex Morgan on the penalty. After conceding each goal, except for the last goal, Vietnam's game did not change by much in terms of chance conceded and pressing intensity. None of the goals conceded came from Vietnam losing the ball in their own half, in spite of Vietnam losing the ball quite often in these areas.

## Narrative

From watching the match, this was a game where Vietnam chose to defend more in a mid block. Vietnam's possessions did not reach far as well, and also Vietnam could not retain their possessions for long.

USWNT got their first goal quite early at the 14th minute coming from a build up sequence, and then right before the 45th minute, USWNT got their chance to score their 2nd goal on penalty, but it was saved. Eventually their 2nd goal came in the 7th minute of stoppage time of the first half through a delayed set piece sequence. Chances kept on piling up onto Vietnam's goal, and the 3rd goal came at the 77th minute, again from another build up sequence. Vietnam did not manage to take any shots to Naeher's goal.

## What could have been done

- Only 1 goal came from a restart, while the other 2 came from moves that were hard to stop against a side of world class quality like USWNT.
- The main improvement was with the ball, as Vietnam could not reach far with their possessions.

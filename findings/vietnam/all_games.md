# All games summary - Vietnam 2023

## The tournament

This was Vietnam's debut at a FIFA Women's World Cup.

Vietnam ended with 3 losses in 3 games, conceding 12 goals and scoring none. After losing the first 2 games, Vietnam were already out with one game in advance. USWNT's 3-0 win against Vietnam in the first game was their only win this tournament, while the Netherlands' 7-0 win in the last game came when the Dutch needed goals to win the group.

## The thread

After the opening match against USWNT, Vietnam played fewer long passes in open play, and against the Netherlands fewer long goal kicks. However, Vietnam lost the ball in their own half about as often against USWNT and Portugal, and most of all against the Netherlands, but were punished more each match via shots and goals conceded.

## How the conceded goals came

7 of 12 goals came from restarts and turnovers, 1 from a low-quality chance, and 4 from quality gap.

While the quality gap decided the result against USWNT, the own half turnovers decided most of the goals conceded in the last two games against the two European opponents.

## What changed

Vietnam conceded more chances per match. In addition, against Netherlands who defended the highest, Vietnam's defensive line was the lowest. While possession share fell from the first match, more of Vietnam's possessions reached their opponents' final third.

## What went well

- Against the two-time defending and record-holding four-time champion USWNT, despite conceding early in the 14th minute, Vietnam managed to save an Alex Morgan penalty and kept the deficit at 1 by the first half stoppage time, and conceding did not change the way they played.
- Vietnam's passing was shorter and more accurate after the opening game.
- More than half of Vietnam's possessions reached Portugal's half in Vietnam's second game.

## What to work on

- As the tournament went on, Vietnam were punished more for turning the ball over in their own half, conceding more chances and, in their last two matches, most of their goals.
- While the ambition of playing shorter passes should be praised, turnovers became more costly.
- Too few chances were created throughout, with none in the first game and 5 shots each in the other two.

# MD2: Vietnam 0-2 Portugal

**Story:** Portugal got 2 quick goals in the first half off winning the ball around the halfway line. Although Vietnam got into the final third more than in the first game against USWNT, they kept giving the ball back, and Portugal had 19 shots in the second half but could not score.

## Context

This was the second match for both teams. Both teams were debutants in this tournament, and both lost their first games in the group stage against the two finalists in 2019.

Portugal ended up drawing with USWNT 0-0 in the last matchday, in which they almost caused an upset in eliminating the two-time defending champion with a close chance at the last minutes of the game. However, this result was not enough for them to advance.

## Turning point

Portugal started aggressively and quickly picked up 2 goals in the 7th and 21st minute. However, despite creating many more chances after, 2-0 ended up being the final score.

## Goals conceded

Both goals came off situations where Vietnam lost the ball near the halfway line. The second goal also came less than 30 seconds off Vietnam's goal kick.

Losing the ball in their own half cost Vietnam far more in this game than their first game against USWNT. Portugal turned many more of these losses into shots, even though they did not defend as highly as USWNT.

## Luck

Portugal scored 2 goals from 3.82 non penalty xG, which suggested that Vietnam were fortunate that they did not have to suffer a heavier defeat.

## Biggest gap

The biggest gap can be seen in both teams' final third, as Portugal were much more dominant in Vietnam's final third both in terms of entries and touches.

## What went well

Despite conceding more shots in the second half, Vietnam did not concede once more. In addition, in 10 minutes after each goal against 10 minutes before each goal, Vietnam did not concede as many shots and expected goals, though the windows were short.

## Narrative

Portugal started quickly in this game, and picked up 2 goals in the 7th and 21st minute after winning the ball in Vietnam's half. In the second half, they did not stop, but instead, they kept on piling chances onto Vietnam's goal. From the 65th to 70th minute, they had multiple chances that added up to approximately 1 expected goal. Nevertheless, the score still ended up being 2-0 for Portugal, which was their first ever win at a FIFA Women's World Cup.

## What could have been done

- Vietnam needed to look at how they turned the ball over in their own half and how they defended after turning the ball over, as both of their goals conceded came after losing the ball in their own half.
- The main improvement was with keeping the ball, as Vietnam got forward more than their first game but kept giving it back quickly. Goal kicks should also be investigated as many of them went long but could not be retained.

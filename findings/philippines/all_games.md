# All games summary - Philippines 2023

## The tournament

This was the Philippines' debut at a FIFA Women's World Cup.

Philippines picked up their first ever Women's World Cup win against co-host New Zealand in their second game. For the other two games, they lost against European opponents Switzerland 2-0 and 1995 champion Norway 6-0. They scored 1 and conceded 8. With the win against New Zealand, they entered the last matchday with qualification still possible.

## The thread

Overall, the Philippines defended more highly than Thailand 2019 and Vietnam 2023, and their defensive line was as high as New Zealand's in their game against New Zealand. Much fewer of the Philippines' turnovers came in their own half, but when it happened, Norway were able to punish the Philippines more than the other two opponents. On the other hand, unlike Thailand and Vietnam's opponents, with the exception of Chile, Switzerland and New Zealand did not defend as highly, which partly explains why the Philippines lost the ball in their own half so rarely. Furthermore, all of Philippines' goal kicks went long, which limited the risk near their own goal and helped keep them in matches. Nevertheless, they could not create much despite reaching the final third more often.

## How the conceded goals came

Among 8 goals conceded, 4 came from restarts and turnovers, 2 from low-quality chances, and 2 from the quality gap.

For the first game against Switzerland, one goal came from a penalty and the other came from the quality gap.

Against Norway, three difficult open play chances converted decided the game in the first half, before three goals coming from restarts and turnovers extended the margin in the second half.

## What changed

The Philippines' approaches for each game were consistent throughout all three games, including defending more highly, not having much of the ball with possession around 30% for all 3 games, and going long from goal kicks. However, the quality of chances they conceded changed by game. Against Switzerland, their second conceded goal came after 3 successive shots accumulating 1.5 expected goals. Against New Zealand, most of the chances they conceded came from set piece situations. Against Norway, difficult chances converted were what decided the game.

## What went well

- Against New Zealand, with their first chance of the game, they converted, while they managed to limit the damage from open play.
- They did not lose the ball often in their own half.
- A higher share of their possessions reached the final third than Thailand 2019 and Vietnam 2023.

## What to work on

- In spite of getting into the final third more often, not many of these entries turned into chances.
- Defending from set pieces could still be improved.
- After going behind against Switzerland, the Philippines only had one more shot. Against Norway, the game was essentially over by half time.

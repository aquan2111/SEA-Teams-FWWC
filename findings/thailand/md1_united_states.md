# MD1: Thailand 0-13 United States

**Story:** Thailand rarely got out of their own half, and a heavy defeat turned into a record-breaking one in the six minutes after half time, when three of four goals came from situations where Thailand lost the ball in their own half.

## Context

This was the first match for both teams. The USWNT came to this tournament as champions from 2015, while 2019 was Thailand's second appearance in a FIFA Women's World Cup. In their debut in 2015, Thailand got one win against Ivory Coast and two 4-0 losses against former champions Germany and Norway.

Eventually the USWNT ended up winning the 2019 World Cup, which was their record-breaking fourth title and second title in a row. They won all their matches in 90 minutes.

## Turning point

Thailand conceded their first goal at the 12th minute, while their third goal was conceded at the 32nd minute, which was also the last goal of the first half.

The other 10 goals of the USWNT were scored in the second half, with 4 goals in 6 minutes from the 50th to 56th minute making it 0-7 and breaking the game. The last 6 goals happened from the 74th minute onwards.

## Goals conceded

Among the 13 goals, 9 came from open play, 2 came from direct set pieces (both from free kicks in Thailand's own half) and 2 from counters. The 2 goals coming from counters are the only 2 chances that came from counters.

For 6 of the 13 goals conceded, it came down to Thailand losing the ball in their own half. 3 others were low-quality chances, with 1 from Rose Lavelle and 2 from Alex Morgan.

## Luck

USWNT scored 13 goals from 5.95 xG, around 7 more than their chances suggested, with 3 being low chance.

The two shots of Thailand accumulated 0.02 xG, in which Thailand's bad luck only changed the margin but not the result.

## Biggest gap

The biggest gap can be seen around Thailand's box, as the USWNT constantly got into it, while Thailand only managed to do the same into USWNT's box twice. Thailand's possessions were much shorter, with most lasting less than 10 seconds. The USWNT's defending locations were much higher up the pitch, while Thailand defended deep in their own half.

## What went well

Thailand still managed to have 2 shots, although they were both quite far from goal.

## Narrative

This was a game where Thailand defended really deep, which allowed the USWNT to dominate territorially in the final third. Thailand's possessions did not reach far as well, and also Thailand could not retain their possessions for long.

The USWNT led by 3 goals at half time, but things turned from bad to worse for Thailand in the six minutes after the break, where USWNT scored 4 more goals, in which 3 came down to Thailand losing the ball in their own half. 6 more goals were scored in the last 16+ minutes of the game, with the 11th and 13th goal coming from Thailand going long for their goal kicks, the ball was then lost in their own half, and USWNT managed to score off counters.

## What could have been done

- Losses in own half must be minimized, as most of the goals came from losing the ball in their own half, specifically with goal kicks as most of them went long and could not be retained within 10 seconds, which started the danger for them.
- Defending from set pieces should be better.
- Even without the goals originating from restarts and turnovers, Thailand would have still lost heavily.

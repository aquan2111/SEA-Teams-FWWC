# All games summary - Thailand 2019

## The tournament

This was Thailand's second appearance at a FIFA Women's World Cup, after making their debut in 2015.

Thailand ended with 3 losses in 3 games, scoring 1 goal and conceding 20. Two of their losses were with heavy margins against two teams that finished with a medal. They were never close to qualifying to the next round, as they were essentially out after losing their first two games against the powerhouses.

## The thread

In every game, the way Thailand lost came differently, although some of the situations could have been better prevented.

For the first game against USWNT, what cost them were losing the ball in their own half, including straight after long goal kicks. For the second game against Sweden, their goals conceded came from restarts, including a free kick, two throw-ins and a penalty. For the last game against Chile, the first goal was an own goal that came after the goalkeeper saved the shot then the ball went around near the goal line, while the second goal came from turning the ball over in Thailand's own half.

## How the conceded goals came

While Thailand conceded differently each game, 11 of 20 goals conceded came from restarts and turnovers, 4 from low-quality chances and 5 from quality gap. Only against the USWNT did the quality gap produce goals in number, while for the other two games, goals mostly came from restarts and turnovers.

## What changed

The difficulty of the games decreased as games went on for Thailand. The overall trends were that Thailand had more of the ball, more possessions reaching the final third, longer possessions, and more goal kicks retained after 10 seconds. However, these trends were hard to separate from their opponents' strengths throughout. Thailand defended much higher in the last game against Chile than in their previous two games.

## What went well

- Against a weaker opponent like Chile, Thailand were willing to defend more highly, and this was the only game that they survived the first half without conceding.
- Thailand kept the ball better after the opening heavy loss against USWNT.
- Thailand scored one goal from a counter against Sweden.

## What to work on

- Restarts at both ends cost Thailand too many goals, including their goal kicks leading to turnover in their own half and set pieces and throw ins against Sweden.
- Losing the ball in their own half happened too often, with stronger opponents being able to capitalize better from these turnovers.
- Thailand could not create many chances from these games, with only around 4 shots per game.

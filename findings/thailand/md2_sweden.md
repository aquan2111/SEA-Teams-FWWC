# MD2: Thailand 1-5 Sweden

**Story:** Sweden decided the match early from the first half with 2 goals from set pieces after 19 minutes. Although Thailand kept the ball better than in the USWNT match, they still rarely got into Sweden's box.

## Context

This was the second match for both teams. Sweden already beat Chile, while Thailand suffered a heavy defeat against USWNT.

Sweden ended up finishing third in the 2019 World Cup, in which their other two losses came in the last group stage match against USWNT (0-2) and the semi final against Netherlands (0-1 after extra time).

## Turning point

Thailand conceded their first goal at the 6th minute, while their third goal was conceded at the 42nd minute, which was also the last goal of the first half.

In the second half, Thailand survived another goal conceded for quite long before conceding the fourth goal at the 81st minute. They got their only goal of the tournament 10 minutes later in the 1st minute of stoppage time, before conceding from a penalty in the 6th minute of stoppage time.

## Goals conceded

Among the 5 goals, 4 came from set piece situations (1 from a free kick, 2 from final third throw ins, and 1 penalty). The other goal was an open play low-quality chance coming from a shot outside the box.

## Luck

Sweden scored 4 non-penalty goals from 3.2 xG, slightly above what their chances have suggested.

The 7 shots of Thailand accumulated 0.29 xG, in which Thailand got 1 goal with an expected goal of only 0.09.

## Biggest gap

The biggest gap can be seen in Thailand's defensive third, in which Sweden had superior number of entries and touches in the final third. Sweden's defending locations were much higher up the pitch, while their pressing intensity was also more aggressive.

## What went well

Thailand got 1 goal out of 7 shots, which was the only goal of this tournament.

In comparison to their defeat against USWNT, they retained the ball for longer, they played fewer long balls, and they completed a higher share of passes under pressure. None of their goals conceded came from losing the ball in their own half.

## Narrative

This was a game where Thailand defended really deep, which allowed Sweden to dominate territorially in the final third.

Sweden led by 3 goals at half time, but their next goal only came at the 81st minute. Thailand got one consolation goal at the 1st minute of added time off a counter attack that ended with a tight-angle shot, but then they still conceded the fifth goal through a penalty 5 minutes later.

Sweden's goals mostly came from restarts, with the first goal coming from a free kick within 6 minutes, followed by a second goal after a throw in at the 19th minute. Their fourth goal also came off a throw in.

## What could have been done

- Defending from restarts and set pieces should be better, given that 2 out of their 4 non-penalty goals came off final third throw ins and 1 came from a direct free kick.
- Sweden were clearly the better side on chances, but the size of the win came from restarts, with only 1 goal coming from open play.

# MD3: Thailand 0-2 Chile

**Story:** Thailand defended higher and held out longer than their first two games, but an own goal straight after the break and a turnover in their own half decided the game, and once behind they did not manage to make any shots.

## Context

This was the third match for both teams. Both teams lost their first two matches against USWNT and Sweden. Since 4 of the 6 best third placed teams would also advance from the group stage, a win was needed for both teams, but Chile could only advance if they won by 3, while that margin for Thailand was 15.

The 2-0 victory was not enough for Chile to advance.

## Turning point

By the end of the first half, neither team managed to score. However, Chile managed to take the lead only 3 minutes into the second half via a Thailand own goal. After this, Thailand did not manage to make any other shots, while Chile did manage to score a 2nd goal at the 78th minute. The score could have been 3-0 if Chile converted their penalty at the end of the match, but it hit the crossbar.

## Goals conceded

The first goal was an own goal, while the second goal came off a loss in Thailand's half followed by a low quality chance.

## Luck

Despite the scoreline being 2-0, Chile only managed to score 1 goal on their own. All their chances accumulated 1.95 expected goals (penalties excluded), which meant Thailand could have conceded more.

Thailand was fortunate to not concede a 3rd goal as Chile's penalty hit the crossbar, but their 1st goal conceded could be seen as unfortunate due to it being an own goal.

In the end, the score matched the chances created.

## Biggest gap

The biggest gap can be seen in the number of times Chile's possessions ended up in Thailand's box and/or a shot. This led to a considerable difference in number of shots created, as Chile attempted 7 times more than Thailand, although such big gap was not as present when it came to shots on target. The gap can also be seen in the expected goals created by both teams.

## What went well

Fewer of Thailand's losses of the ball happened in their own half, even though one still lead to a goal conceded. They managed to concede later in the game. Thailand also defended much higher up the pitch in comparison to their two previous games, and although their defensive line was still lower than Chile's, the gap is much smaller.

## Narrative

Chile was a weaker opponent than both the USWNT and Sweden, and while they still dominated Thailand, such dominance was not as strong.

After the first half, Chile got their 1st goal via a Thailand own goal. After this, Chile kept on creating chances and got their second goal at the 78th minute. Chile's chance rate rose after going 2-0 up, as they chased the 3rd goal they needed, and ended up with a penalty during the last minutes of the game. However, it hit the crossbar, meaning the 2-0 victory was not enough for Chile to go through.

## What could have been done

- Both goals came from situations that Thailand had a part in, an own goal coming from a saved Chile shot and a turnover in their own half, against an opponent who still created far more chances.
- After conceding the first goal, Thailand was more proactive as they had more of the ball and pressed more aggressively, but they could not convert into creating chances, which was what the match needed. However, they did not had as much of the ball after conceding the second goal.
